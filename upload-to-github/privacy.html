Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Fly Me to the Brew ("we," "us," "our") operates this website to share information about our coffee stand located inside the YMCA at 9055 Northlake Pkwy, Orlando, FL 32827. This page explains what happens when you visit.

What this site collects

This website is informational — it does not have accounts, checkout, or online ordering. We do not collect names, emails, or payment information through this site.

  • If you tap a phone number or "Call to Order" link, your device places a normal phone call — we don't receive any data from that click itself.
  • If you click "Get Directions" or use the embedded map, you're taken to or shown content from Google Maps, which operates under its own privacy policy.
  • Standard web server logs (like IP address and browser type) may be recorded automatically by our hosting provider, as with virtually any website, for security and reliability purposes.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies or use tracking scripts. The embedded Google Map may set cookies controlled by Google — see Google's privacy policy for details.

Third parties

We link out to a few third-party services you may choose to visit:

  • Google — for Maps/directions
  • Instagram — our social page

We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites once you leave ours.

Children's privacy

This site is a general business information page and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If anything about how this site works changes — for example, if we add online ordering in the future — we'll update this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Call us at (757) 679-1032, or stop by the stand at 9055 Northlake Pkwy, YMCA Lobby, Orlando, FL 32827.
